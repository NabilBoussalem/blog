# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Home cooks and food lovers browsing on phones and laptops for polished, trustworthy recipes they can discover, save locally, and share with friends or family.

## Product Purpose
Create a fast, search-friendly recipe blog that makes discovery effortless, turns individual recipes into practical cooking guides, and encourages repeat visits through local saved recipes and simple sharing.

## Brand Personality
Elegant, clean, and approachable. The tone should feel refined without feeling sparse, with confidence carried by strong photography, readable typography, and calm pacing.

## Anti-references
Avoid crowded layouts that overwhelm the recipe content. Avoid ultra-minimal treatments that feel empty, cold, or generic.

## Design Principles
- Let imagery and typography do the brand work, so recipes feel desirable before they feel instructional.
- Keep discovery friction low, so visitors can move quickly from browsing to cooking, saving, or sharing.
- Balance polish with practicality, so the interface feels refined but never precious.
- Favor calm hierarchy and consistent structure, so every recipe is easy to scan on mobile and desktop.
- Build trust through clarity, speed, and accessible defaults rather than decorative effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use normal accessible web defaults with clear contrast, semantic structure, keyboard support, and reduced-complexity interactions so the site remains comfortable on common devices and screen sizes.
