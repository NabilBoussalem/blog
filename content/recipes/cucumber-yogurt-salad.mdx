ingredients:
  - 2 large cucumbers, thinly sliced
  - 1 cup Greek yogurt
  - 2 tablespoons chopped dill
  - 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  - 1 teaspoon olive oil
  - Pinch of sea salt
instructions:
  - Combine yogurt, dill, lemon juice, and salt in a bowl.
  - Fold in cucumber slices and coat gently.
  - Chill for 20 minutes before serving.
---

<Tip>Salt cucumbers for 5 minutes and pat dry for extra crunch.</Tip>

This bright cucumber salad pairs well with grilled proteins or pita wraps.
