ingredients:
  - "400 g rigatoni"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "3 cloves garlic, sliced"
  - "1 teaspoon chili flakes"
  - "500 ml tomato passata"
  - "120 ml double cream"
  - "50 g grated parmesan"
instructions:
  - "Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until al dente."
  - "Warm the olive oil, garlic, and chili flakes in a large pan."
  - "Stir in passata and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the cream and parmesan, then toss with the pasta."
  - "Adjust the texture with pasta water and serve immediately."

A creamy tomato sauce is one of those pantry miracles that always feels a little luxurious. The sauce clings to every ridge of pasta and comes together with just enough richness to feel comforting without becoming heavy.

## Make it your own

Add roasted peppers, spinach, or buttery white beans when you want to stretch the meal a little further.
