## Ingredients

- 1 large eggplant, diced
- 2 medium zucchini, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped
- 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon herbes de Provence
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the vegetables.** Wash and cut all vegetables into similar-sized pieces for even cooking.

2. **Sauté the onion.** Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes.

3. **Add garlic and peppers.** Stir in the garlic and bell peppers, cooking for another 3 minutes.

4. **Add the eggplant.** Toss in the eggplant and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

5. **Add zucchini and tomatoes.** Add the zucchini and diced tomatoes. Season with herbes de Provence, salt, and pepper.

6. **Simmer.** Reduce heat to low, cover, and let the ratatouille simmer for 30–35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until all vegetables are tender.

7. **Serve.** Garnish with fresh basil leaves. Serve warm as a main course with crusty bread, or as a side dish.

## Chef's Tips

- For a more elegant presentation, slice the vegetables into thin rounds and layer them in a baking dish before roasting in the oven at 190°C (375°F) for 45 minutes.
- Ratatouille tastes even better the next day as the flavors meld together.
- This dish pairs wonderfully with a glass of rosé from Provence.
