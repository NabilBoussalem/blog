Ratatouille is the kind of dish that fills the kitchen with the scent of late-summer vegetables and olive oil.

## Ingredients

- 1 eggplant, diced
- 2 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 yellow bell pepper, chopped
- 1 onion, finely sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 ripe tomatoes, chopped
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Fresh basil, for serving

## Instructions

1. Warm the olive oil in a large sauté pan and cook the onion for 4 minutes until soft.
2. Add the eggplant and peppers, then cook for 8 minutes so the vegetables begin to caramelize.
3. Stir in the zucchini, garlic, tomatoes, thyme, salt, and black pepper.
4. Lower the heat and simmer for 25 minutes, stirring from time to time, until the vegetables are tender and silky.
5. Taste, adjust the seasoning, and finish with torn basil before serving.

## Serving idea

Serve with crusty bread, grilled fish, or simply spooned over rice for an easy French-inspired supper.
