ingredients:
  - "1 zucchini, sliced"
  - "1 red pepper, chopped"
  - "1 aubergine, cubed"
  - "3 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "200 g couscous"
  - "250 ml hot vegetable stock"
  - "1 handful chopped parsley"
instructions:
  - "Roast the vegetables with olive oil and salt at 220°C for 30 minutes."
  - "Cover the couscous with hot stock and rest for 10 minutes."
  - "Fluff the couscous with a fork."
  - "Fold in the roasted vegetables and parsley."
  - "Season with lemon juice and extra olive oil before serving."

Couscous is one of the easiest ways to turn a tray of roasted vegetables into a generous meal. The grains soak up every bit of dressing, while the vegetables bring sweetness and color.
