Quiche Lorraine is a timeless French tart that is equally welcome at brunch, lunch, or a casual supper.

## Ingredients

- 1 shortcrust pastry shell
- 150 g smoked bacon lardons
- 4 eggs
- 250 ml crème fraîche
- 125 ml milk
- 100 g Gruyère, grated
- Pinch of nutmeg
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Blind bake the pastry shell for 15 minutes at 190°C.
2. Cook the bacon in a skillet until just crisp, then drain on paper towels.
3. Whisk the eggs, crème fraîche, milk, Gruyère, nutmeg, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
4. Scatter the bacon over the pastry shell.
5. Pour the custard filling over the bacon and bake for 30 to 35 minutes until the center is just set.
6. Let the quiche rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

## Serving idea

Serve warm with mustard-dressed greens and a glass of chilled white wine.
