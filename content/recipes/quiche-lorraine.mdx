## Ingredients

### For the Pastry
- 250 g all-purpose flour
- 125 g cold butter, cubed
- 1 egg yolk
- 3–4 tablespoons ice water
- 1 pinch of salt

### For the Filling
- 200 g lardons or thick-cut bacon, diced
- 3 large eggs
- 200 ml heavy cream
- 100 ml whole milk
- 150 g Gruyère cheese, grated
- 1 small onion, finely diced (optional)
- Freshly grated nutmeg
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. **Make the pastry.** In a food processor, pulse the flour, butter, and salt until the mixture resembles coarse breadcrumbs. Add the egg yolk and ice water, pulsing until the dough just comes together. Wrap in plastic and refrigerate for 30 minutes.

2. **Roll and blind bake.** Roll the dough on a floured surface and fit it into a 23 cm tart pan. Prick the bottom with a fork. Line with parchment paper and fill with baking weights. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 12 minutes. Remove the weights and bake for 5 more minutes until lightly golden.

3. **Cook the lardons.** Fry the bacon until crispy. If using onion, cook it in the bacon fat until softened.

4. **Prepare the custard.** Whisk together the eggs, cream, and milk. Season with nutmeg, salt, and pepper.

5. **Assemble.** Spread the cooked lardons (and onion, if using) over the pastry base. Sprinkle with half the Gruyère. Pour the custard over the top and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.

6. **Bake.** Reduce oven temperature to 180°C (350°F) and bake for 30–35 minutes, until the filling is set and golden on top.

7. **Rest and serve.** Let the quiche rest for 10 minutes before slicing. Serve warm or at room temperature with a simple green salad.

## Chef's Tips

- For an even flakier crust, use a mix of butter and a little lard in the pastry.
- The authentic Quiche Lorraine from the Lorraine region traditionally does not include cheese, but most modern versions do.
- This quiche can be made ahead and reheated gently in the oven.
