title: "Honey Yogurt Pancakes"
description: "Tender yogurt pancakes drizzled with honey and topped with warm fruit for slow weekend mornings."

category: "Breakfast"
difficulty: "Easy"
prepTime: "10 minutes"

totalTime: "25 minutes"
servings: 4
tags: ["breakfast", "pancakes", "honey", "brunch"]
featured: true
publishedAt: "2026-02-02"
notes:
  - "Do not overmix the batter; a few lumps keep the pancakes light."
  - "Top with orange zest, figs, or berries depending on the season."
ingredients:
  - "200 g plain yogurt"
  - "2 eggs"
  - "2 tablespoons honey"
  - "180 g self-raising flour"
  - "120 ml milk"
  - "Butter or olive oil, for the pan"
instructions:
  - "Whisk yogurt, eggs, honey, and milk until smooth."
  - "Fold in the flour until just combined."
  - "Cook spoonfuls of batter in a lightly greased pan over medium heat."
  - "Flip when bubbles appear and cook until golden on both sides."
  - "Serve warm with extra honey."
---

These pancakes have the gentle tang of yogurt and just enough sweetness to make breakfast feel special. They are fluffy, tender, and particularly lovely with fruit that has been warmed in a pan with a spoonful of honey.
