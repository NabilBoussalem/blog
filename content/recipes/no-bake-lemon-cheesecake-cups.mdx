ingredients:
  - 1 cup crushed graham crackers
  - 2 tablespoons melted butter
  - 8 oz cream cheese
  - 1/2 cup yogurt
  - 1/4 cup maple syrup
  - 2 tablespoons lemon juice
instructions:
  - Mix graham crumbs and butter, then divide into jars.
  - Beat cream cheese, yogurt, maple syrup, and lemon juice until smooth.
  - Spoon filling into jars and chill for at least 2 hours.

<Tip>For extra citrus aroma, top with fresh lemon zest before serving.</Tip>
