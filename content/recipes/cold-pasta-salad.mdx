ingredients:
  - 12 oz cooked short pasta, cooled
  - 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  - 1 cup diced cucumber
  - 1/3 cup olives
  - 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
instructions:
  - Whisk lemon juice and olive oil in a large bowl.
  - Add pasta, tomatoes, cucumber, and olives.
  - Mix well and chill for at least 30 minutes.
