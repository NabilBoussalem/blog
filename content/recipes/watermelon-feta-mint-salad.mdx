ingredients:
  - 4 cups watermelon cubes
  - 1/2 cup crumbled feta
  - 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves
  - 1 tablespoon lime juice
  - 1 teaspoon olive oil
instructions:
  - Toss watermelon with mint, lime juice, and olive oil.
  - Sprinkle feta over top and chill for 10 minutes.
  - Serve immediately while cold.
---

<Callout>Use cold watermelon straight from the fridge for best texture.</Callout>
