These delicate crêpes are quick to make and endlessly adaptable for breakfast, dessert, or an afternoon treat.

## Ingredients

- 250 g plain flour
- 4 eggs
- 500 ml milk
- 2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Extra butter, for the pan

## Instructions

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and salt in a mixing bowl.
2. Add the eggs, milk, melted butter, and vanilla, then whisk until the batter is smooth.
3. Rest the batter for 15 minutes if time allows for more tender crêpes.
4. Lightly butter a non-stick pan over medium heat.
5. Pour in a small ladle of batter and swirl the pan to create a thin layer.
6. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, flip, and cook for 30 seconds more.
7. Repeat with the remaining batter and keep the crêpes warm under a clean towel.

## Topping suggestions

Try them with orange marmalade, chocolate spread, or classic melted butter with lemon juice and sugar.
