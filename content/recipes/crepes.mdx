## Ingredients

- 250 g all-purpose flour
- 3 large eggs
- 500 ml whole milk
- 50 g melted butter, plus extra for cooking
- 2 tablespoons sugar (for sweet crêpes)
- 1 pinch of salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Make the batter.** In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and eggs. Gradually add the milk while stirring to avoid lumps. Mix in the melted butter, sugar, salt, and vanilla extract.

2. **Rest the batter.** Cover the bowl and let the batter rest in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes. This allows the flour to fully hydrate and produces more tender crêpes.

3. **Heat the pan.** Heat a crêpe pan or non-stick skillet over medium heat. Lightly brush with melted butter.

4. **Cook the crêpes.** Pour a small ladleful of batter into the center of the pan, quickly tilting and swirling to spread it into a thin, even circle.

5. **Flip when ready.** Cook until the edges start to lift and the bottom is golden (about 1–2 minutes). Flip with a spatula and cook the other side for about 30 seconds.

6. **Stack and serve.** Place cooked crêpes on a plate, stacking them. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.

## Serving Suggestions

- **Sweet:** Nutella, fresh strawberries, whipped cream, lemon juice and sugar, or caramelized apples.
- **Savory:** Ham and gruyère cheese, smoked salmon and crème fraîche, or mushrooms and béchamel sauce.
- **Classic Parisian:** Simply fold with butter and a squeeze of lemon, then dust with powdered sugar.
