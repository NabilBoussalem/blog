  - "Preheat the oven to 200°C."
  - "Mix olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, oregano, and salt in a large bowl."

  - "Bake for 35 minutes until golden and cooked through."
  - "Scatter with parsley before serving."

This is the kind of dinner that fills the kitchen with the scent of lemon peel and warm herbs before anyone even reaches the table. It is simple enough for a Wednesday evening, but polished enough to serve when friends linger over wine and olives.

## Serving idea

Spoon the pan juices over fluffy couscous and add a salad of cucumber, dill, and red onion for a dinner that feels complete without much extra effort.
