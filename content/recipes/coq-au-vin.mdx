## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken (about 1.8 kg), cut into pieces
- 200 g lardons or thick-cut bacon, diced
- 1 bottle (750 ml) good Burgundy red wine
- 250 g button mushrooms, quartered
- 12 pearl onions, peeled
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 bouquet garni (thyme, bay leaf, parsley)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Brown the lardons.** In a large Dutch oven, cook the lardons over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside.

2. **Sear the chicken.** Season the chicken pieces with salt and pepper. Brown them in the bacon fat on all sides, about 4 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.

3. **Sauté the aromatics.** Add the pearl onions, carrots, and garlic to the pot. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes.

4. **Build the sauce.** Sprinkle flour over the vegetables and stir for 1 minute. Add the tomato paste and stir well.

5. **Deglaze with wine.** Pour in the entire bottle of red wine, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom. Add the bouquet garni.

6. **Braise.** Return the chicken and lardons to the pot. Bring to a simmer, then cover and cook on low heat for 60 minutes.

7. **Add mushrooms.** Sauté mushrooms in butter separately until golden, then add to the pot. Cook for another 15 minutes.

8. **Finish and serve.** Remove the bouquet garni. Adjust seasoning. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve with boiled potatoes or crusty bread.

## Chef's Tips

- Use a wine you would enjoy drinking — the quality of the wine directly affects the flavor of the dish.
- Marinating the chicken overnight in the wine with herbs makes the flavor even more intense.
- This dish reheats beautifully and is even better the second day.
