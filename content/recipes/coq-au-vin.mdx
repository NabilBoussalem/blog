Coq au Vin turns a simple chicken supper into a slow-cooked French classic with deep savory flavor.

## Ingredients

- 6 chicken thighs
- 150 g smoked bacon, diced
- 12 pearl onions, peeled
- 250 g mushrooms, halved
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 500 ml red wine
- 250 ml chicken stock
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken and brown it in a Dutch oven until golden on both sides, then set aside.
2. Cook the bacon until crisp, then sauté the pearl onions and mushrooms in the rendered fat.
3. Stir in the garlic and flour, cooking for 1 minute to remove the raw taste.
4. Pour in the wine and stock, then whisk in the tomato paste.
5. Return the chicken to the pot with the thyme and bay leaf.
6. Cover and simmer gently for 50 minutes until the chicken is tender and the sauce is glossy.
7. Taste, season, and serve with mashed potatoes or buttered noodles.

## Chef's note

A Burgundy-style red wine gives the sauce depth, but any dry red you enjoy drinking works beautifully.
