difficulty: Easy
calories: 180
ingredients:
  - 5 ripe tomatoes
  - 1 cucumber
  - 1 red bell pepper
  - 1 garlic clove
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
  - 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
instructions:
  - Blend all ingredients until smooth.
  - Season with salt and chill for 1 hour.
  - Serve cold with diced cucumber topping.
