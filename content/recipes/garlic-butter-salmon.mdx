notes:
  - "Pull the salmon from the oven when it still flakes softly in the center."
  - "Serve with greens, potatoes, or herby rice."
ingredients:
  - "4 salmon fillets"
  - "50 g butter, melted"
  - "2 cloves garlic, grated"
  - "1 lemon, sliced"
  - "1 tablespoon chopped dill"
  - "Sea salt and black pepper"
instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 190°C."
  - "Arrange the salmon in a lined baking dish."
  - "Mix melted butter with garlic and spoon over the fish."
  - "Top with lemon slices and bake for 18 minutes."
  - "Finish with dill and black pepper."

Salmon is one of those dependable ingredients that always feels a little graceful. A quick garlic butter and a few lemon slices are all it needs to become dinner.
