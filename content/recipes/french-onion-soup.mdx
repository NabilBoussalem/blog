French Onion Soup rewards patience with sweet caramelized onions and an aromatic broth worthy of a Parisian bistro.

## Ingredients

- 4 large onions, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon flour
- 120 ml dry white wine
- 1 litre beef or vegetable stock
- 4 slices baguette
- 120 g Gruyère, grated
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter with the olive oil in a heavy pot.
2. Add the onions and cook over medium-low heat for 35 to 40 minutes, stirring often, until richly caramelized.
3. Stir in the garlic and flour, then cook for 1 minute.
4. Deglaze with the white wine, scraping up any browned bits.
5. Pour in the stock, add the thyme, and simmer for 20 minutes.
6. Toast the baguette slices until crisp.
7. Ladle the soup into ovenproof bowls, top with bread and Gruyère, then broil until the cheese is melted and golden.

## Serving idea

Pair it with a crisp green salad for an elegant lunch or light dinner.
