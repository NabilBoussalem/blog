notes:
  - "Let the salad sit for 10 minutes so the chickpeas absorb the dressing."
  - "Crumbled feta makes it even more satisfying."
ingredients:
  - "2 cans chickpeas, drained"
  - "1 cucumber, diced"
  - "200 g cherry tomatoes, halved"
  - "1 small red onion, finely sliced"
  - "2 tablespoons chopped dill"
  - "Juice of 1 lemon"
  - "3 tablespoons olive oil"
instructions:
  - "Combine the chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, onion, and dill in a serving bowl."
  - "Whisk together lemon juice and olive oil."
  - "Pour the dressing over the salad and season well."
  - "Toss gently and rest briefly before serving."

This salad tastes like the kind of lunch you want after a morning at the market: crisp vegetables, creamy chickpeas, and enough herbs to keep every bite bright.

> Pack it into jars for weekday lunches, or pile it next to grilled fish for an easy dinner spread.
