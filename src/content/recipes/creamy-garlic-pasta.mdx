## Ingredients

- 12 ounces pasta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 5 garlic cloves, finely sliced
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3/4 cup grated parmesan
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 handful parsley, chopped
- Salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta until just al dente.
2. Warm the olive oil in a wide skillet, then gently cook the garlic until fragrant and lightly golden.
3. Pour in the cream and bring it to a low simmer. Stir in the parmesan, lemon zest, black pepper, and a splash of pasta water.
4. Add the drained pasta and toss until every strand is coated in the glossy sauce.
5. Finish with parsley and extra parmesan before serving.

## Notes

This is a perfect weeknight recipe when you want something cozy in under half an hour. Add wilted spinach or peas for an easy variation.
