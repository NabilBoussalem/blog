ingredients:
  - "4 salmon fillets (about 150g each), skin-on"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "3 tablespoons unsalted butter"
  - "4 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 lemon, juiced and zested"
  - "2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill"
  - "Salt and freshly ground black pepper"
  - "Lemon wedges for serving"
instructions:
  - "Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels and season generously with salt and pepper on both sides."
  - "Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat."
  - "Place the salmon skin-side up and sear for 4 minutes until a golden crust forms. Flip carefully."
  - "Reduce heat to medium. Add butter and garlic to the pan, swirling until the butter melts and the garlic is fragrant."
  - "Spoon the garlic butter over the salmon repeatedly for 2–3 minutes until the fish is cooked through."
  - "Remove from heat, squeeze lemon juice over the fillets, and top with fresh dill and lemon zest. Serve with lemon wedges."
---

This garlic butter salmon is one of those recipes that looks and tastes like you spent hours in the kitchen, but it comes together in just 25 minutes. The rich garlic butter sauce makes every bite absolutely irresistible.

**Tip:** Don't move the salmon after placing it in the pan — let it develop a proper crust before flipping. Serve with steamed asparagus, rice pilaf, or a simple arugula salad.
