## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 3/4 cup plain yogurt
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- Orange zest

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a loaf tin.
2. Whisk together the dry ingredients in one bowl.
3. In another bowl, whisk the eggs, yogurt, olive oil, honey, vanilla, and orange zest.
4. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet until just combined.
5. Bake until golden and a tester comes out clean, about 40 to 45 minutes.

## Serving suggestion

Finish the cooled cake with a spoonful of honey and a dollop of whipped yogurt.
