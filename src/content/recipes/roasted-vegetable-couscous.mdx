ingredients:
  - "1½ cups couscous"
  - "1½ cups boiling vegetable broth"
  - "1 red bell pepper, diced"
  - "1 zucchini, diced"
  - "1 eggplant, diced"
  - "1 red onion, cut into wedges"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "1 teaspoon cumin"
  - "1 teaspoon smoked paprika"
  - "½ cup crumbled feta cheese"
  - "¼ cup chopped fresh parsley"
  - "2 tablespoons lemon juice"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Toasted pine nuts for garnish"
instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper."
  - "Toss the bell pepper, zucchini, eggplant, and red onion with olive oil, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper."
  - "Spread the vegetables in a single layer on the baking sheet. Roast for 25–30 minutes, stirring halfway, until tender and lightly charred."
  - "Meanwhile, place the couscous in a bowl and pour the boiling broth over it. Cover tightly and let sit for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork."
  - "Combine the fluffy couscous with the roasted vegetables, parsley, and lemon juice. Toss gently."
  - "Top with crumbled feta, toasted pine nuts, and an extra drizzle of olive oil before serving."
---

This roasted vegetable couscous is a vibrant, satisfying dish that works beautifully as a main course or a hearty side. The smoky roasted vegetables and bright lemon vinaigrette make every bite sing.

**Tip:** Feel free to swap in any seasonal vegetables you have on hand — sweet potato, butternut squash, and cherry tomatoes all work wonderfully. Leftovers are excellent served cold as a salad the next day.
