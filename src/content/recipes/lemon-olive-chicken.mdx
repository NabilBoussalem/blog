## Ingredients

- 6 chicken thighs
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 1 lemon, sliced
- 3/4 cup green olives
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken and sear it in olive oil until golden on both sides.
2. Add the onion and garlic to the pan and cook until soft.
3. Pour in the stock, then add lemon slices, olives, and oregano.
4. Cover and simmer gently until the chicken is tender and the sauce is glossy.
5. Spoon the braising juices over the top and serve warm.

## Notes

Serve with crusty bread, couscous, or the saffron rice pilaf for a complete dinner spread.
