## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds salmon fillet
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 lemon, sliced
- 1 orange, zested
- 2 tablespoons chopped dill
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 1 small garlic clove, grated
- Flaky salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a baking tray with parchment.
2. Place the salmon on the tray and rub with olive oil, orange zest, garlic, dill, parsley, salt, and pepper.
3. Arrange lemon slices on top and roast until the fish flakes easily, about 16 to 18 minutes.
4. Rest for a few minutes before serving with grains or greens.

## Serving suggestion

Pair this salmon with a crisp salad and warm bread for a dinner that feels polished without much effort.
