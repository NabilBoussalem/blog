## Ingredients

- 2 cups basmati rice
- 3 tablespoons butter or olive oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 1/3 cup toasted almonds
- 1 pinch saffron steeped in 2 tablespoons hot water
- 3 cups vegetable stock
- Salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear.
2. Sauté the onion in butter until deeply golden and sweet.
3. Stir in the rice, almonds, and saffron water, then pour in the stock.
4. Bring to a boil, cover, and cook on low heat until tender.
5. Rest for 10 minutes, then fluff gently before serving.

## Notes

The pilaf works beautifully beside roasted meats, grilled vegetables, or a simple spoonful of yogurt.
