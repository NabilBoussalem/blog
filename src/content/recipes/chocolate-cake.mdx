## Why This Recipe Works

The secret to this incredibly moist chocolate cake is an unexpected ingredient: hot coffee. It doesn't make the cake taste like coffee — instead, it deepens the chocolate flavor and creates an incredibly tender crumb.

## Baking Tips

- **Don't be alarmed by the thin batter.** The hot coffee makes the batter very liquid, but this is what gives the cake its amazing moisture.
- **Use Dutch-process cocoa** for the richest flavor, though natural cocoa works too.
- **Room temperature ingredients** (eggs, buttermilk) help the batter mix evenly.
- Let the cakes cool completely before frosting, or the ganache will melt.

## Variations

- **Chocolate Cupcakes:** Fill cupcake liners 2/3 full and bake for 18–22 minutes.
- **Mocha Cake:** Add 1 tablespoon instant espresso powder to the dry ingredients.
- **Mint Chocolate:** Add 1/2 teaspoon peppermint extract to the batter.

> **Storage:** This cake stays moist for up to 3 days at room temperature, or up to 1 week in the refrigerator. Bring to room temperature before serving for the best texture.
