  - 250 grams plain flour
  - 70 grams Dutch-process cocoa powder
  - 2 teaspoons baking powder
  - 1 teaspoon bicarbonate of soda

  - 300 grams caster sugar
  - 3 large eggs
  - 180 milliliters olive oil
  - 240 milliliters buttermilk
  - 180 milliliters hot coffee
  - 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 250 grams dark chocolate, melted and cooled
  - 220 grams unsalted butter, softened
  - 320 grams icing sugar
instructions:
  - Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl until evenly combined.
  - Beat the eggs, sugar, olive oil, buttermilk, coffee, and vanilla until smooth, then fold into the dry mixture.
  - Divide the batter between two lined cake tins and bake until the centers spring back lightly.
  - Beat the butter and icing sugar until pale, then blend in melted dark chocolate for a smooth frosting.
  - Cool the cake layers fully before frosting and finishing with berries or shaved chocolate.

## Texture notes

Hot coffee deepens the cocoa flavor without making the cake taste like coffee. Olive oil keeps the crumb plush for days, which makes this a reliable make-ahead cake for gatherings.

## Frosting tips

If the frosting starts to look loose, chill it for ten minutes and beat again. If it feels too firm, add a spoonful of warm cream until it loosens to a spreadable texture.

> Use a serrated knife to level the cake layers only after they are completely cool. Warm cake will tear and drag crumbs into the frosting.

## Serving ideas

This cake is rich enough to serve with a spoonful of crème fraîche or a bowl of lightly macerated strawberries. For a cleaner finish, leave the sides semi-naked and let the frosting gather generously on top.
