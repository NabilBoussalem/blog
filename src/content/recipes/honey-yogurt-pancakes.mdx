ingredients:
  - "1½ cups all-purpose flour"
  - "2 teaspoons baking powder"
  - "¼ teaspoon salt"
  - "1 cup Greek yogurt"
  - "½ cup milk"
  - "2 large eggs"
  - "3 tablespoons honey, plus more for serving"
  - "2 tablespoons melted butter"
  - "1 teaspoon vanilla extract"
  - "Fresh berries and pistachios for topping"
instructions:
  - "In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt."
  - "In a separate bowl, mix the Greek yogurt, milk, eggs, honey, melted butter, and vanilla extract until smooth."
  - "Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined (a few lumps are fine)."
  - "Heat a non-stick pan or griddle over medium heat and lightly butter the surface."
  - "Pour ¼ cup of batter per pancake onto the pan. Cook until bubbles form on the surface, about 2–3 minutes, then flip and cook for another 1–2 minutes."
  - "Serve stacked with fresh berries, a sprinkle of pistachios, and a generous drizzle of honey."
---

These pancakes are incredibly fluffy thanks to the Greek yogurt. The subtle tang pairs beautifully with sweet honey and fresh fruit.

**Tip:** For extra-fluffy pancakes, don't overmix the batter — lumps are your friend! You can also add a pinch of cinnamon or cardamom for a warm spice note.
