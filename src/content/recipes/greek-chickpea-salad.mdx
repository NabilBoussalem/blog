publishedAt: "2026-03-05"
ingredients:
  - "2 cans (400g each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed"
  - "1 large English cucumber, diced"
  - "2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved"
  - "½ red onion, thinly sliced"
  - "½ cup Kalamata olives, halved"
  - "150g feta cheese, crumbled"
  - "¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil"
  - "2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice"
  - "1 teaspoon dried oregano"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh mint leaves for garnish"
instructions:
  - "In a large bowl, combine the chickpeas, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, red onion, and olives."
  - "In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt, and pepper."
  - "Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently to combine."
  - "Top with crumbled feta cheese and fresh mint leaves."
  - "Serve immediately or refrigerate for 30 minutes to let the flavors meld."
---

This Greek chickpea salad is perfect for meal prep, picnics, or a quick healthy lunch. It's packed with protein and Mediterranean flavors.

**Tip:** This salad tastes even better the next day after the dressing has had time to soak in. Store in the fridge for up to 3 days.
