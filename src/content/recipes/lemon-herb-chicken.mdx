ingredients:
  - "4 bone-in chicken thighs"
  - "3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil"
  - "3 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 large lemon, juiced and zested"
  - "1 teaspoon dried oregano"
  - "1 teaspoon dried thyme"
  - "½ teaspoon smoked paprika"
  - "Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste"
  - "Fresh parsley for garnish"
instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F)."
  - "In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, garlic, lemon juice, lemon zest, oregano, thyme, and paprika."
  - "Season the chicken thighs generously with salt and pepper, then coat evenly with the lemon herb marinade."
  - "Place the chicken skin-side up in a baking dish and pour any remaining marinade over the top."
  - "Bake for 35 minutes until the skin is golden and the internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F)."
  - "Let rest for 5 minutes, then garnish with fresh parsley and serve with lemon wedges."
---

This is one of our most-loved recipes — the kind of dish you'll come back to again and again. The combination of bright lemon, aromatic herbs, and golden chicken skin makes it a true crowd-pleaser.

**Tip:** For extra flavor, marinate the chicken for at least 30 minutes (or overnight) before baking. Serve alongside roasted potatoes or a fresh green salad for a complete Mediterranean meal.
