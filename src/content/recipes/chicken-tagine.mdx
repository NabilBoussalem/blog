## About This Dish

Chicken tagine is one of the most beloved dishes in Moroccan cuisine. Named after the conical clay pot it's traditionally cooked in, this dish combines tender chicken with a fragrant blend of spices, preserved lemons, and briny olives.

## Tips for the Best Tagine

- **Use bone-in chicken** for the most flavorful and tender results.
- **Don't skip the saffron** — even a small pinch adds incredible depth and color.
- **Preserved lemons** are the secret ingredient. You can find them at Middle Eastern grocery stores or make your own.
- Let the tagine simmer low and slow for the best texture.

## Serving Suggestions

This tagine pairs beautifully with fluffy couscous, warm flatbread, or steamed basmati rice. A simple side salad of cucumber, tomato, and fresh herbs rounds out the meal perfectly.

> **Cook's Note:** If you don't have a tagine pot, a Dutch oven works just as well. The key is a tight-fitting lid to trap moisture and flavor.
