## Ingredients

- 8 chicken thighs, bone-in and skin-on
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
- 2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1/2 cup dried apricots, halved
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Toasted almonds for garnish

## Instructions

### Step 1: Season the Chicken

Pat chicken thighs dry and season generously with salt, pepper, cumin, and turmeric.

### Step 2: Sear the Chicken

Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven or tagine over medium-high heat. Sear chicken skin-side down for 4-5 minutes until golden. Flip and cook 2 more minutes. Remove and set aside.

### Step 3: Build the Base

In the same pot, add onion and cook for 5 minutes until softened. Add garlic, ginger, cinnamon, and coriander. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

### Step 4: Simmer

Add diced tomatoes, chicken broth, dried apricots, and honey. Stir to combine. Nestle the chicken pieces back in, skin-side up. Bring to a simmer.

### Step 5: Cook Low and Slow

Cover and reduce heat to low. Cook for 40-45 minutes until chicken is tender and falling off the bone. The sauce should be thick and glossy.

### Step 6: Serve

Serve over fluffy couscous, garnished with fresh cilantro and toasted almonds.

## Tips

- Bone-in, skin-on thighs give the best flavor and stay moist during braising.
- You can substitute dried apricots with dried figs or dates.
- This dish tastes even better the next day as the flavors develop overnight.
