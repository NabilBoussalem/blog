## Ingredients

- 3 lbs Roma tomatoes, halved
- 1 head of garlic, top cut off
- 1 large onion, quartered
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves
- 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Heavy cream for drizzling (optional)
- Crusty bread for serving

## Instructions

### Step 1: Roast the Vegetables

Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Arrange halved tomatoes, onion quarters, and garlic head on a baking sheet. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Roast for 35-40 minutes until caramelized and tender.

### Step 2: Prepare for Blending

Let the roasted vegetables cool slightly. Squeeze the roasted garlic cloves out of their skins.

### Step 3: Blend

Transfer roasted tomatoes, onion, and garlic to a blender or large pot (if using an immersion blender). Add vegetable broth, fresh basil, balsamic vinegar, and sugar. Blend until smooth.

### Step 4: Heat and Season

Pour the soup into a pot (if you used a blender) and heat over medium heat until warmed through. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt, pepper, and more vinegar if needed.

### Step 5: Serve

Ladle into bowls. Drizzle with a swirl of cream and garnish with fresh basil leaves. Serve alongside warm crusty bread.

## Tips

- Roasting the tomatoes concentrates their sweetness and adds depth.
- The sugar and balsamic vinegar balance the acidity — adjust to your taste.
- This soup freezes beautifully for up to 3 months.
